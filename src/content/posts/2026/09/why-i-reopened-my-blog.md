Two years ago, I created a personal blog with WordPress by using an open-source theme. I wrote about organic chemistry and inorganic chemistry as I thought I had found explanations that were easier to understand than those in textbooks. My naive understanding of science led me to share these intuitive but crude explanations with other students, because I thought they might help others. However, it attracted only a dismal readership, and most of them were bots, according to Cloudflare. Eventually, I decided to shut it down to save money. After all, I had to spend over 240 yuan a year maintaining the server even if there were no visitors at all.  

Over the past two years, I have learned a lot about the natural sciences, including biology, chemistry and physics. I gradually realized how naive my understanding of science had been. Although some explanations were easier to understand than those in textbooks, that accessibility sometimes came at the cost of rigor and accuracy. Such explanations should not be presented without clearly stating their limitations. That is also why I refuse to republish those articles on my new blog. I must take responsibility for what I publish.  

Aside from that, I have studied some computer science. With help from AI, I can now design and build my own website. So I chose to build this new site with Astro. It does have a source of inspiration, but I don’t want to reveal it directly. There is only one hint: turn on dark mode and take a look at the homepage.  

For now, I am studying physical chemistry, inorganic chemistry and data structures and algorithms. Writing is also a form of teaching, and teaching others often helps the teacher understand the material more deeply. Personal experiences are unique, so they are undoubtedly worth writing down. It is also a good way to practice my English. That is why I have chosen to reopen my blog and share what is worth sharing.
